What is Adobe Stardust?

Stardust is a skill in the public adobe/skills repo. It is not a product. It builds on impeccable, the 57K-star UX design skill library that does the actual design work, and it ends inside an Edge Delivery Services (EDS) site.

How does the skill work?

  1. Extract. The skill reads its inputs and writes down what it found.
  2. Direction. It proposes a design direction.
  3. Prototype. The human gate. Review happens here, before anything is built.
  4. Build into EDS. The approved prototype becomes blocks in the EDS site.
